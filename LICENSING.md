# Licensing

Autokarta is **source-available**, not open source. It is published under the
[PolyForm Small Business License 1.0.0](LICENSE.md).

## Free use

You may use, modify and self-host Autokarta for free if your company has
**fewer than 100 people** (employees and contractors together) **and less than
1,000,000 USD (2019, inflation-adjusted) revenue** in the prior tax year.
Affiliated companies are counted together.

In addition to the license, the licensor grants permission to use Autokarta
for **personal, educational and non-profit purposes**.

## Commercial license

Larger companies, and anyone who wants to offer Autokarta as a hosted service
to third parties, need a commercial license. Open an issue or contact the
maintainer via GitHub: https://github.com/Eretuk

## Contributions

Contributors sign a [Contributor License Agreement](CLA.md) on their first
pull request. It lets the project keep offering both the free and the
commercial license.
